Warning Signs

Things to look out for

⏰
Artificial urgency

“Act now or your account will be blocked.”

🎁
Unbelievable rewards

Unexpected prizes, refunds, or easy-money offers.

🔑
Requests for secrets

Passwords, PINs, OTPs, or recovery codes.

🔗
Suspicious links

Misspelled domains, unfamiliar websites, or unexpected attachments.

Scam awareness feed

Know the tricks.

Explore example scam patterns and the steps you can take to protect yourself. These are educational examples, not live incident reports.

High risk pattern🏦

Fake Bank Verification

A message claims your bank account will be suspended unless you provide an OTP, PIN, or password.

Never share OTPs or PINs.
High risk pattern💳

Payment & QR Scams

Someone claims you must scan a QR code or enter your UPI PIN to receive money.

You generally don't need a UPI PIN to receive money.
Be cautious💼

Fake Job Offers

A recruiter promises easy income but asks for registration fees, deposits, or sensitive documents.

Verify the employer independently.
High risk pattern💻

Fake Tech Support

A pop-up or caller says your device is infected and demands payment or remote access.

Use official support channels.
Be cautious🎁

Prize & Lottery Scams

You are told you won something you never entered for, but must pay a fee to claim it.

Don't pay to claim unexpected prizes.
High risk pattern👤

Impersonation Scams

A fake account copies someone you know and asks urgently for money or private information.

Verify through another trusted channel.

Cyber safety academy

Build your digital defense.

Simple habits can help you avoid common online threats. Open each lesson to learn more.

🔐

Password Security

Protect your accounts with strong, unique passwords.

Read lesson ↗

Use a different password for every important account. A password manager can help. Turn on multifactor authentication when available, and never share verification codes.

🎣

Spot Phishing

Recognize fake messages and misleading websites.

Read lesson ↗

Check the full sender address and domain. Be careful with unexpected attachments, urgent requests, and links. Open the official website yourself instead of using a suspicious message link.

📱

Phone Safety

Keep your device and applications protected.

Read lesson ↗

Install updates, review app permissions, use a screen lock, and download apps from trusted sources. Avoid installing unknown apps at someone's request.

💳

Digital Payments

Make online payments with care.

Read lesson ↗

Never share your UPI PIN, OTP, or banking password. Check the recipient and amount before approving a transaction. Be suspicious of requests to install remote-access apps.

🌐

Privacy Protection

Think carefully about what you share online.

Read lesson ↗

Limit public personal information. Review social media privacy settings. Don't post addresses, passwords, identity documents, or live location details publicly.

🧠

AI & Deepfake Awareness

Don't trust every voice, video, or screenshot.

Read lesson ↗

AI can imitate voices and images. Verify unusual requests for money or secrets using a separate trusted contact method. A realistic-looking video is not proof by itself.

Know where to get help.

If you suspect a scam, preserve relevant evidence, contact the affected service through official channels, and use the appropriate reporting service.

🇮🇳 India Cybercrime Reporting

For suspected cybercrime in India, visit the official National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal. The national helpline 1930 is intended for reporting financial cyber fraud.

Official Cybercrime Portal ↗ Ministry of Home Affairs ↗
If money has been lost to suspected financial cyber fraud, contact 1930 promptly and follow the official reporting instructions. This website does not contact authorities for you.
